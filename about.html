Meet Mala — The Heart and Soul Behind Kismet

Mala is a natural connector. She is intuitive, warm, and deeply people-focused. Guided by strong empathy and a genuine passion for bringing like-minded people together, she founded Kismet Matchmaking to help singles find meaningful, lasting relationships.

With a background in design and business development, Mala has spent decades cultivating authentic connections and creating experiences that are intentional and rooted in trust. She blends emotional intelligence with strategic vision, transforming ideas into meaningful connections and lasting impact.

For over 30 years, Mala has built trusted, enduring networks within the South Asian community across the U.S. Since launching Kismet in 2025, she has already created dozens of intentional, high-quality matches, each one a reflection of her heart-led approach to matchmaking.

Why Matchmaking Matters

Dating is a vital part of discovering compatibility and building meaningful relationships. Mala encourages intentional dating and follows a simple framework that helps clients understand the natural progression of a relationship.

While dating apps can help you meet people, they often lack accountability, structure, and depth. A skilled matchmaker understands your goals, curates introductions with care, and provides guidance to nurture lasting connections.
